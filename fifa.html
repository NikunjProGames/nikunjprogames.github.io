World Cup Soccer Caps

Play FIFA World Cup Soccer Game On NPG

Step onto the pitch for a clever tactical challenge! World Cup Soccer Caps is an exciting, turn-based football game where you launch your soccer puck caps across the field to strike the ball into your opponent's net. Pick your favorite international team and outsmart the defense across multiple tactical formats.

Exciting Game Modes

  • Score Mode: A classic match format where the first player to score 3 goals claims victory. No clock restrictions!
  • Time Mode: A racing clock challenge! You have exactly 3 minutes to score as many points as possible to secure the win.
  • Tournament: Battle through consecutive matches against global teams as you claw your way up to the tournament finals.
  • 2-Player Mode: Share your keyboard or screen layout with a friend and take turns testing each other's goalie lines.

Controls & How to Play

  • Select Team: Pick your chosen country flag from the menu and press the arrow to start the match.
  • Aim and Fire: Wait for your soccer puck caps to light up during your active turn. Click and hold the puck you want to utilize.
  • Set Your Power: Drag your mouse pointer or finger backward away from your puck. An indicator line will reveal your trajectory path.
  • Release: Release your mouse click or finger tap to shoot. Control your shot speed precisely to dodge opposing caps and strike goals cleanly.
