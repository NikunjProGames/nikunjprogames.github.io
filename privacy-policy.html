Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

Welcome to PleyZ, operated and maintained by PleyZ. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information that may be collected or stored when you use the PleyZ website, games, user accounts, and related features.

In short: PleyZ uses Firebase to provide account functionality and preserve your PleyZ Score and recent games. We only intend to process information that is reasonably necessary to provide and improve PleyZ and its features.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, PleyZ may process the following types of information.

1.1 Account and Authentication Information

When you create or sign in to a PleyZ account, Firebase Authentication may process information associated with your account, such as:

  • Email address associated with your account.
  • Unique Firebase user identifier.
  • Display name or profile information, where provided.
  • Profile photo, where provided through an authentication provider.
  • Authentication provider information.
  • Information required to maintain your signed-in account session.

PleyZ may provide sign-in options through Firebase Authentication, including Google Sign-In and other authentication methods enabled on the website.

1.2 PleyZ Score and Gameplay Information

The main purpose of the PleyZ account system is to preserve account-based gaming progress and features so that users do not lose their information when they return to PleyZ.

Depending on the features available, we may store information such as:

  • Your PleyZ Score.
  • The number of games you have played.
  • Your recently played games.
  • Game-related statistics or progression.
  • Other information required for account-based PleyZ features.

This information is associated with your PleyZ account so that your progress and relevant features can be maintained across sessions and devices where supported.

1.3 Future Personalized Game Recommendations

PleyZ may introduce personalized game recommendations in the future. If this feature is implemented, PleyZ may use information about games you have previously played on PleyZ to suggest other games that may be relevant to your interests.

Any such feature will be implemented in accordance with the privacy practices and applicable requirements in effect when the feature is introduced.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through PleyZ may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and maintaining PleyZ user accounts.
  • Providing authentication and sign-in functionality.
  • Preserving your PleyZ Score so that it is not lost.
  • Saving and displaying your recently played games.
  • Providing other account-based gameplay features.
  • Improving the website, games, and overall user experience.
  • Detecting and preventing unauthorized activity, abuse, cheating, or security problems.
  • Identifying and resolving technical bugs and errors.
  • Understanding general usage of PleyZ where necessary for operating and improving the platform.
  • Providing advertisements where applicable.
  • Complying with applicable legal and security requirements.

3. Firebase Services

PleyZ uses Google Firebase to provide important account and gameplay functionality.

Firebase Authentication is used to create and manage PleyZ user accounts and provide sign-in functionality. Firebase can maintain information such as a user's unique identifier and account information required for authentication.

PleyZ may also use Firebase database services, such as Cloud Firestore, to store account-related information such as PleyZ Score, games played, recent games, and other information required for PleyZ features.

Firebase is a third-party service operated by Google. Its processing of information is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

At the time of this Privacy Policy, PleyZ does not intentionally use cookies or similar tracking technologies to build personal profiles of users.

However, third-party services used by PleyZ, including authentication, advertising, or embedded games, may use their own cookies, local storage, or similar technologies as part of their services.

Because these technologies may be controlled by third parties, their use may be governed by the respective third party's privacy policy and settings.

5. Advertising

PleyZ may display advertisements through third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense.

Advertising providers may use their own technologies, including cookies or similar technologies, to provide, measure, personalize, or limit advertisements, depending on the user's location, settings, and applicable policies.

PleyZ does not directly control the independent data practices of third-party advertising providers.

6. Third-Party Games and Services

PleyZ provides access to games and content that may be created, hosted, embedded, or delivered by third-party developers, publishers, distributors, or other providers.

When you interact directly with a third-party game or service, that provider may process information according to its own privacy policy.

PleyZ does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party services and encourages users to review the applicable privacy information when interacting with them.

7. Information Sharing

PleyZ does not intend to sell users' personal information as a standalone product.

Information may be processed by service providers where reasonably necessary to operate PleyZ and provide its features. These may include:

  • Authentication and account service providers.
  • Cloud database and infrastructure providers.
  • Advertising providers.
  • Security and technical service providers.
  • Third-party game and content providers.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect users or PleyZ, or investigate security and abuse incidents.

We take reasonable technical measures to help protect information associated with PleyZ accounts against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or loss.

Account and gameplay information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide PleyZ features, preserve account functionality, maintain security, and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

When information is no longer reasonably required for a legitimate purpose, it may be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements.
