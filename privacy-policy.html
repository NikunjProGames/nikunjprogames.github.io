Some games, advertisements, or analytics services available on NPG are provided by third-party networks. These third parties may collect data independently according to their own respective privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party content you interact with.

NPG is a casual gaming platform. We do not knowingly or intentionally collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any revisions or updates will be immediately active upon posting to this page. We encourage you to check back periodically to stay informed.
