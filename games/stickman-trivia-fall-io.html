About Stickman Trivia Fall IO

Stickman Trivia Fall io takes your trivia skills to the next level! Challenge your brain and reflexes as you compete against friends and players worldwide. Test your knowledge in Solo Mode, progress through fun quests, and beat the Boss to earn rewards. Love multiplayer? Jump into the Arena to battle up to four players, climb leaderboards, and score big! Play Classic one-on-one matches, enjoy weekly and monthly special quizzes, and customize your stickman avatar. Hundreds of trivia questions across multiple categories make this the ultimate addictive quiz & trivia game!

How to Play

Double left-click on the correct answer text to move your stickman there
