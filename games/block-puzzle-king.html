About Block Puzzle King

Block Puzzle King is a classic elimination game. Its adorable graphics prevent eye strain, and varied level difficulties keep it engaging. The game maintains freshness while offering abundant props for players to strategically use.

How to Play

1.Drag the bottom blocks to fill the gaps on the board. 2.Complete a horizontal or vertical line to clear those blocks. 3.Clear all blocks to pass the level.
