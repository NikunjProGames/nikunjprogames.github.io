About Speed per Click: Obby

"Speed per Click: Obby" – An exciting clicker-speed simulator in the style of obby and blocky games!

Controls

✅ Dynamic clicker – Earn speed with every click and accelerate to incredible levels!

✅ Pets – Buy pets to increase speed per click and beat levels faster!

🔥 Ready for the challenge? Click, jump, and become the champion of Speed per Click: Obby!

Key Features

  • ✅ Obby-style challenges – Overcome parkour tracks with jumps and tricky maneuvers, just like in obby games!
  • ✅ Progress & rewards – Collect wins, unlock new locations, and upgrade your skills!
  • ✅ Rebirth system – Perform rebirths to gain bonuses and speed up again!
  • 🌍 Play in the best traditions of obby and blocky parkour – It has everything: clicker mechanics, parkour, adrenaline, and fun!

How to Play

Computers: - WASD - control the character. - Space - jump. Mobile devices: - Joystick - control the character. - Button in the lower right corner - jump. - To rotate the camera, swipe the screen.
