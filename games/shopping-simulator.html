About Shopping Simulator

Earn game money by tapping the hamster, raise the customer's level and buy everything from the store shelves. Anime, horror, food, pets, household goods, hundreds of heroes in one place! Buy anything you want! More than 500 unique items, 200 leveling levels, lots of mini-games and lots of emotions are waiting for you!

How to Play

Earn game money in different mini-games to upgrade your level! Tap the hamster and complete the puzzles to get as many as possible! Unlock new levels to become the best!
