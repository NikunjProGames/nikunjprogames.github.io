About My Phone Store

My Phone Store is a fun and casual game about creating and selling tech devices!

Build smartphones, laptops, and tablets, improve your production, and sell devices to customers. Grow your shop, earn money, and turn a small store into a successful electronics business.

Key Features

  • Simple and addictive gameplay; Create phones, laptops, and tablets.
  • Sell devices and earn money; Upgrade your store and expand your business.
  • Play, earn, and build the best gadget store!

How to Play

To control, you need to press the WASD keys or use a mouse if you're playing on a computer. If you're playing on a phone, you need to use your finger.
