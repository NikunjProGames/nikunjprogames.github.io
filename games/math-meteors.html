About Math Meteors

Math Meteors is a mental-math arcade. Meteors drift down towards the planet, each carrying a math problem. Type the answer on the keypad and press ✓ — the meteor with that answer explodes. A meteor that reaches the surface costs a life; you have three.

Problems get harder as the round goes on: addition and subtraction within ten, multiplication and division, numbers up to a hundred, then two-step problems and brackets — new kinds appear gradually. The sky breathes: calm stretches, busier ones and, once a minute, a ten-second Apocalypse with a storm of meteors. Later some meteors split into two or three small ones, and now and then a boss arrives — a big violet meteor with a chain of problems, each answer breaking one of its parts. Three difficulty levels change how fast and how often meteors come. Lost? From time to time (at most once every five minutes) you can watch an ad to continue the round instead of starting over.

Eight achievements mark the milestones — the first meteor down, 500 and 2000 points in a round, twenty hits in a row without a mistake, fifty in Times tables, a thousand on Hard — and every one of them puts a badge into your collection. Come back the next day for the daily reward: three, seven and thirty days in a row each add a star, and the streak sits next to your name.

Controls

One tap to play: the round starts right away with your last mode and difficulty (the very first time, the planet asks which mode and difficulty to train).

Desktop: Play in portrait or landscape — with a mouse, a touch screen or a computer keyboard.

Key Features

  • Answers are typed, not picked — no guessing, only counting; Eleven tiers of problems — from sums within ten to brackets with two-digit numbers — and three difficulty levels.
  • Times tables mode: multiplication 2…9 only, the sky just gets busier — with its own records and leaderboard; A global and a personal table for each of the four modes. Difficulty multiplies the score — Easy ×1, Normal ×2, Hard ×4.
  • Gold for every round, more for a clean Apocalypse and a beaten boss; spend it in the shop on three traps: ice freezes the sky, reverse sends the meteors back up so they crash into each other, blast clears the whole field; Splitting meteors, bosses with multi-step problems and the Apocalypse once a minute.
  • Eight achievements with badges, a daily reward and streak stars — all in one collection; Pause, volume and language in the settings; interface in 7 languages.

How to Play

1. Press "Play" — the round starts at once with the last mode and difficulty (the first time, the planet asks you to pick them). To change them, use the mode tiles and the difficulty switch under "Play" or on the result screen: all problems, + −, × ÷ or times tables; Easy, Normal or Hard. 2. Solve the problem and type the answer on the keypad — on the right in landscape, at the bottom in portrait: digits, ⌫ deletes, ✓ submits. On a computer keyboard use digits, Backspace and Enter. 3. The meteor with the typed answer explodes and scores: every period of the round raises the multiplier (×2 → ×16), and difficulty multiplies it again — Easy ×1, Normal ×2, Hard ×4. 4. A meteor that reaches the planet takes one of three lives. Lose them all and the round is over. At most once every five minutes you are offered "Watch an ad to continue" first: the ad brings one life back and clears the sky, your score stays. 5. On the result screen switch the mode or the difficulty if you like and press "Play again", or return to the main menu. 6. The pause button in the corner of the keypad lets you continue, open the settings (volume and language) or return to the main menu. 7. Every round goes to the leaderboard of its mode by itself; the "Leaderboard" button shows the global and the personal table of each mode. 8. Every round pays gold. Spend it in the "Shop" (or press the wallet at the top right) on traps, or watch a video there for more gold. In a round use a trap with its button over the keypad or the keys Q (ice), W (reverse) and E (blast). Ice holds the sky still, reverse sends the meteors back up — the fast ones crash into the slow ones — and blast destroys every meteor on the field. 9. The "Achievements" button in the menu lists all eight achievements with what each one is awarded for. They unlock by themselves as you play — a badge lands in the collection under your name. Once a day the menu offers the daily reward: take it, and the streak grows; three, seven and thirty days in a row each add a star. Tip: shoot the meteor that will land first — the fast ones have a long tail. When the countdown 3 · 2 · 1 starts, get ready for the Apocalypse.
