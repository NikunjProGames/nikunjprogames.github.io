Game Info & Controls

Sure Shot is the online multiplayer in which you are a soldier dropped into the madness of warfare. Lace up your boots and get ready soldier as you will be pitted against another special ops team where you will play different game modes to find the ultimate squadron across the globe. You can choose to play with your friends in team deathmatch or play on your own in a free for all type mode. Web Dev https://www.toyourlead.com/ Objective of both is to defeat as many enemies as possible to finish with the highest score. There are plenty of big maps to play on. Good luck!
