Q: Is Special Ops free to play?
A: Yes, the game is 100% free to play without any downloads or installations required.

Mechanics & Progression

A: You earn in-game credits by completing matches, finishing daily quests, and climbing the leaderboard. Use these credits in the store to purchase new firearms and unique weapon skins.
