ABOUT PLEYZ

GAMES
WORTH PLAYING.

PleyZ is an independent gaming platform built around one simple idea: finding games should be just as exciting as playing them.

Instead of trying to become a place with thousands of random games, PleyZ focuses on building a curated collection of games that are actually worth discovering.

From indie creations to carefully selected games from trusted developers and distributors, PleyZ is creating a modern home for browser gaming — made for players who want something new.
