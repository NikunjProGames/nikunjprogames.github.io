About Me & NPG

Welcome to Nikunj Pro Games (NPG)! I am Nikunj, a 15 year old (as of 2026) front-end developer passionate about creating a clean, fast, and completely unblocked space to play classic web games. I manage, update, and optimize this entire arcade platform by myself to keep it running smoothly for players all over the world.
